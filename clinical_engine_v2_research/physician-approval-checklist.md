# Physician approval checklist

## Scope
- [ ] Population, age, setting and out-of-scope conditions match intended clinic use.
- [ ] Eligibility is separate from the clinical condition.
- [ ] Pregnancy, sex and age behavior is correct where relevant.
- [ ] Unknown/not-asked is never interpreted as no.

## Data and safety
- [ ] Critical facts are explicitly listed.
- [ ] Freshness limits are clinically acceptable.
- [ ] Allowed patient-reported/unverified data are identified.
- [ ] Allergy, pregnancy, renal/hepatic function, duplication and interactions are executable where relevant.
- [ ] Red-flag suppression behavior is acceptable.
- [ ] A safety-rule failure causes visible abstention.

## Recommendation
- [ ] It is suggestion-only and cannot mutate medication/order/diagnosis automatically.
- [ ] Action type and required confirmation are correct.
- [ ] Duplicate/conflict semantic key is clinically appropriate.
- [ ] Explanation identifies used and missing data.

## Evidence/local adaptation
- [ ] Source title, version and exact locator have been independently rechecked.
- [ ] Evidence certainty and recommendation strength are not conflated.
- [ ] Iranian formulary/availability and local policy are reviewed.
- [ ] Adaptation rationale, clinical owner and review date are recorded.

## Validation
- [ ] Positive, negative, boundary, missing, stale, conflict and red-flag cases pass.
- [ ] Shadow differences from v1 are classified.
- [ ] Alert burden and interruptiveness are acceptable.
- [ ] Rollback/suspension criteria are agreed.
